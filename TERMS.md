## 1. What Naruhodo is

Naruhodo translates Japanese manga shown in your browser into Traditional Chinese. Text detection and recognition run on your computer; the recognized text is then translated by Anthropic's AI models. You can translate in one of two ways:

- **Hosted Translation**: you sign in with your email address and use the Naruhodo service. New accounts get free pages; after that, a paid subscription is needed.
- **Own API Key**: you use your own Anthropic API key. Your use of Anthropic's API is governed by your own agreement with Anthropic, and these terms' sections on accounts, free pages and subscriptions do not apply.
